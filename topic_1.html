Topic 1: The History of the URL

Sir Tim Berners-Lee

Fourty years ago, the advancement of technology and its applications was soaring. One such advancement was the creation of the World Wide Web. This creation was to answer the cumbersome nature of passing around information around computers. One such man, by the name of Sir Time Berners-Lee found the answer to this problem.

Working at the European Council for Nuclear Research, Sir Berners-Lee decided to develop a way to obtain these pieces of info across computers in a uniform manner. This was done through the creation of the World Wide Web in 1989. With his invention of the World Wide Web, came three foundamental pillars to its creation...

  • Hyper Text Markup Language
  • Hypertext Transfer Protocol
  • Uniform Resource Locator

Of these three is the URL, the topic of Discussion for this research website. The URL as its name suggests was a means to locate resources/addresses on the web. The URL is a unique structure that is composed of six parts that make up the URL. These parts are what create the address for resources like files, videos, photos, etc., to be found on the internet.
